# About Toonstalk

Toonstalk is a place to **hang out, talk anime, and actually feel people around you**.

Not just comments.  
Not just likes.  
Real-time rooms where fans show up, move around, and chat together.

Whether you’re here to scream about the latest episode, share wild theories, post memes, or just chill while someone else types nonsense — Toonstalk is built for **being present**, not just scrolling.

Anime is more fun when it’s shared.  
Toonstalk exists for that exact reason.

---

## Why Toonstalk Exists

Most social apps today are great at one thing: **keeping you scrolling**.

But anime fans don’t just want to consume content —  
we want to **react together**, talk live, and meet people who get the hype.

Toonstalk brings that feeling in a modern way:

- Jump into themed rooms
- See who’s around _right now_
- Chat instantly, no pressure, no performance

It’s less “post and wait”  
and more “yo, come here, this part was crazy”.

---

## Not Just for Fans — Also for Creators

Toonstalk isn’t only for watching and reacting.

It’s also a space for **creators, artists, and fans who love to build things**.

If you:

- Draw anime or manga-inspired art
- Design characters, OCs, or fan art
- Enjoy creating and sharing your own worlds

You can **create your own rooms**, customize them, and **upload your work** for others to see, react to, and talk about _live_.

No algorithms deciding who gets seen.  
No pressure to “go viral”.  
Just people who are actually there, right now.

---

## What Makes Toonstalk Different

- 🎮 **Live rooms**, like game lobbies for anime fans
- 🧍 **Avatars & movement**, because it feels better than static chats
- 💬 **Real-time conversations**, not delayed replies
- 🎨 **Creator-friendly spaces**, where your art has a place to live
- 🌱 **Chill & welcoming**, whether you talk a lot or just lurk

You can be loud.  
You can be quiet.  
You can create.  
You can just vibe.

All of it’s okay here.
